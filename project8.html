Grandma's Home



Grandma's Home is a multiplayer party game developed in Unity, designed for groups of friends who enjoy competing against each other, as well as streamers.
Furthermore, the game is designed to offer maximum replayability.


Lore

The game takes place after a witch-necromancer grandmother decides to experiment in order to communicate with her husband and her dog, whom she lost too soon.
Following these experiments, she only manages to bring back her dog, Nestor.
She doesn't realize that she has also accidentally summoned mischievous little ghosts, who, while hiding from humans, try to destroy the house.
Since their grandfather's death, his grandchildren often visit the family home to keep their now-widowed grandmother company.
It is there that they discover the existence of the ghosts.
In order to neutralize them and protect the house, the children decide to make makeshift weapons from slime found in the house and equip their children with water pistols.


Game Play

The children protect the house by searching for ghosts and repairing sabotaged objects before their grandmother returns from taking Nestor for a walk
The ghosts, for their part, must use cunning to fulfill their objectives, failing to destroy the house themselves, they can deceive the children into destroying valuable objects in the house whose appearance they have taken.
